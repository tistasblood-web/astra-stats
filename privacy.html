Astra Stats Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

What the app accesses

Astra Stats uses YouTube API Services with read-only permissions. After a channel owner signs in with Google, it reads that channel's public details, video list and YouTube Analytics statistics.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show performance statistics and reports to the channel owner. The app cannot change anything on YouTube.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens and results are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by Astra Stats. The data is not sold or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

By using this app, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can remove this app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Deleting the app's local files removes the stored data.
